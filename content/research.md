<div class="paper">
  <div class="paper-venue">Post-Communist Economies, 2025</div>
  <div class="paper-title"><a href="https://doi.org/10.1080/14631377.2025.2566418">Unveiling the Shadows: Tracing the Informal Economy in the Balkans from 1996 to 2021</a></div>
  <div class="paper-desc">Using the MIMIC model, we analyse the informal economy in ten Balkan countries from 1996 to 2021. Results show a decline from approximately 35% in 1996 to over 29% of GDP by 2021, with the COVID-19 pandemic causing a 1–3 percentage point increase in 2020. With Asllani & Schneider.</div>
