<div class="paper">
  <div class="paper-venue">Post-Communist Economies, 2025</div>
  <div class="paper-title"><a href="https://doi.org/10.1080/14631377.2025.2566418">Unveiling the Shadows: Tracing the Informal Economy in the Balkans from 1996 to 2021</a></div>
  <div class="paper-desc">Using the MIMIC model, we analyse the informal economy in ten Balkan countries from 1996 to 2021. Results show a decline from approximately 35% in 1996 to over 29% of GDP by 2021, with the COVID-19 pandemic causing a 1–3 percentage point increase in 2020.</div>
</div>

## Working Papers

<div class="paper">
  <div class="paper-title"><a href="https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4945628">East London Citizen Prosperity Index Methodology</a></div>
  <div class="paper-desc">The Citizen Prosperity Index, developed by the Institute for Global Prosperity at UCL, offers a novel approach to measuring prosperity beyond traditional economic indicators. This methodology paper outlines the CPI's application in east London, focusing on 15 Lower Layer Super Output Areas across five boroughs undergoing regeneration projects.</div>
</div>

<div class="paper">
  <div class="paper-title"><a href="https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4973048">The Educational Path to Institutional Quality: A Panel Analysis of Developing Economies</a></div>
  <div class="paper-desc">This study examines how education influences the quality of institutions in developing nations. Utilising a comprehensive panel dataset comprising 86 developing nations from 1970 to 2014, a range of econometric methods is applied, including Fixed Effects, Difference-GMM, System-GMM, and bias-corrected estimators.</div>
</div>

<div class="paper">
  <div class="paper-title"><a href="https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4453614">Crime and Unemployment – The Effects of Labour Market Policies and Shadow Economy</a></div>
  <div class="paper-desc">We examine the crime-unemployment relationship in 26 EU countries and the possibility of potential asymmetries developing in the presence of labour market policies and/or certain structural characteristics.</div>
</div>

<div class="paper">
  <div class="paper-title"><a href="https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4982819">Education and Institutional Quality: Evidence from EU Regions</a></div>
  <div class="paper-desc">This study examines the determinants of institutional quality across European regions, focusing on the roles of education and income. Using a cross-sectional dataset of 192 NUTS 1 and NUTS 2 regions from 28 EU member states.</div>
</div>

<div class="paper">
  <div class="paper-title">A Model of Institutional Change</div>
  <div class="paper-desc">This paper presents a dynamic model that explains the evolution and transition of political regimes over time, focusing on a dictator government's optimisation problem.</div>
</div>
