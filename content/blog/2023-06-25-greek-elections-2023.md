---
title: "Greek Elections 2023: The Victory of New Democracy, but Democracy's Loss"
date: 2023-06-25
---

In the 2023 Greek elections, New Democracy emerged victorious, but the triumph was overshadowed by the loss of democracy.

The Greek elections held in June 2023 were characterised by low turnout and the emergence of three far-right parties, which collectively secured 13% of the vote. The recent election results have marked a significant setback for Greek democracy, despite New Democracy's triumph as the party with the highest percentage of votes.

Three far-right parties have made an unprecedented entrance into the Greek Parliament, marking a significant development in the ongoing elections. Three political parties have made significant strides in the country's political landscape, gathering a combined percentage of 13% and establishing a notable presence among voters. The entry highlights concerns regarding the presence of both tolerance and extremism in specific segments of our society.

The most worrisome element of the ongoing elections is the strikingly low number of voters who have turned up to cast their ballots. The latest voter turnout has been a cause of concern, with the participation rate dropping to 50%. This trend is indicative of a growing sense of disillusionment and disinterest among the public towards the political system. The aforementioned is a concerning sign for the well-being of our democratic system and the participation of individuals in moulding the destiny of our country.

New Democracy has achieved its goal of victory, however, the consequences of this electoral process require careful examination. The recent emergence of far-right parties in Parliament has prompted a call for analysis and action from responsible authorities to safeguard democratic values and fundamental principles.

In the face of a concerning surge in right-wing backing, political education and advocating for democratic principles, political engagement, and social unity is imperative. In order to address the potential rise of far-right movements, it is imperative to promote awareness and encourage communication between political entities, citizens, and social groups. This will help to effectively tackle the underlying social issues that may contribute to the growth of such movements.

The June 2023 Greek elections have sounded a significant alarm regarding the state of our democracy. In order to preserve and advance the fundamental principles that serve as the foundation of our democratic system, it is imperative that each and every one of us take action and demonstrate unwavering commitment. To achieve a more equitable and comprehensive future for everyone, it is imperative that we prioritise awareness, active participation, and unity.
